3+ Years turning ideas into websites

I started learning web development as part of my BS in Computer Science — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, out of pure curiosity for how websites actually came together.

That curiosity didn't stay a class assignment for long. Building and customizing websites for real clients became something I genuinely looked forward to, and over time it grew into a freelance career I've run alongside my studies ever since.

What makes me different is that I don't just build a website. I focus on responsive design, clean user experience, and local SEO — so my clients' sites don't just look good, they actually perform and get found.

My mission is to help businesses and individuals turn their ideas into professional, functional websites that grow their online presence.
